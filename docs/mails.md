# Mails

When the tool sends mails and to whom. **Members** of a circle are the
people holding a role in it, also as Circle Lead or Circle Rep of a
sub-circle. **"Everything"** means the people who chose the mails "About
everything in all circles" in their settings.

## Proposals

| When | Who gets a mail |
|---|---|
| A proposal is published | The circle's members, "everything", the proposer |
| The proposer takes it back to a draft | The same |
| The proposer withdraws it | The same |
| It can't be adopted async and waits for a meeting (an objection, a conflict) | The same |
| It's adopted async (by the last consent or at the deadline) | The same, and the Role Leads of roles it removes or moves away, and the Secretaries and Circle Leads of sub-circles it moves roles or policies into or out of |
| A question or comment | The proposer |
| The proposer answers | Who asked or commented (still members of the circle) |
| Somebody asks for a meeting | The proposer and the circle's Secretary (else the Facilitator, else the Circle Leads) |
| The circle or role it belongs to is removed (also drafts) | The proposer |

Who wrote a response doesn't get it. Consents send nothing, the mail
comes when the proposal is adopted. When nobody else has to
consent (e.g. a role's only Role Lead adding roles inside), publishing
adopts it right away: only the mail about adopting.

## Roles and domains

| When | Who gets a mail |
|---|---|
| A role's policy for its domains is added, changed or removed | The members of the circle and of its sub-circles, "everything", who changed it |
| The Circle Leads change the circle's strategy (each time it's saved) | The same |
| You get or lose a role (assigning, removing, an election) | You, about all your changes at once |
| People join or leave a circle (through roles they get or lose) | The circle's Secretary |
| Your focus in a role changes | You |

These mails and those about policies for domains name who changed them, unless it was you.

## Meetings

| When | Who gets a mail |
|---|---|
| A meeting ends, with outputs or notes | The attendees, the circle's members, "everything"; for adopted proposals also the Role Leads of roles they changed, removed or moved away, and the Secretaries and Circle Leads of sub-circles they move roles or policies into or out of |
| Elections are due (Mondays) | The Facilitator (else the Secretary, else the Circle Leads) of each circle with terms ending within 14 days, unfilled elected roles (not the Circle Rep, which may stay unfilled) or roles held without a term |

Proposals adopted and elections held in a meeting come with its outputs
when it ends ("End the meeting", or three hours after the last
activity), not one by one.

## No mails

- Drafts and tensions, until published.
- People who left the company (inactive) get nothing, also as proposers.

## How

One plain text mail per person, in the language of their page,
greeting you by your first name and linking this page at the end. Mails
go out once the change is saved. They can't be unsubscribed one by one,
filter them in your mail program. The administrators may have set up an
address getting a copy of every mail, saying whom the mail went to.
