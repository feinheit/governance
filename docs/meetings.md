# Meetings

You plan meetings in the calendar. In the tool a meeting begins when
somebody starts it; the tool asks those who aren't the circle's
Secretary (or standing in for them) to confirm. Everybody follows on a
shared screen or their own and adds agenda items from their own device;
the page updates by itself.

- **Who leads**: the meeting's secretary has the buttons: steps,
  processing items, objections, adopting, elections, attendees, ending
  the meeting; they also change proposals. It's the circle's Secretary,
  if missing a stand-in (the Facilitator, then the Circle Leads,
  constitution 5.3.6). "Take over" next to the secretary makes somebody
  else the secretary, e.g. while the Secretary is late; the tool asks
  those who aren't acting as the circle's Secretary to confirm.
- **Following**: the others see the current step and agenda item
  highlighted, a summary of what the proposal changes (updated while the
  secretary amends it), and open it with "Open the proposal".
  In tactical meetings they look at another review (checklists, metrics,
  updates) on their own screen by clicking it, "Back to the meeting"
  returns.
  While you type or have something open, the page doesn't update; a
  notice at the top says so.
- **Notes** at the bottom: what doesn't belong to an agenda item, e.g. a
  special meeting's intent. The secretary writes them, they save
  themselves; the outputs mail includes them.
- **Attendees** ("Attendees" beside the agenda) are the circle's members
  at the start; the secretary invites others or removes them there.
  "End the meeting" is at the bottom of the agenda; with unprocessed
  items it asks first.
- **One meeting per circle**: starting a second one leads to the running
  one.
- **End**: "End the meeting", or by itself three hours after the last
  change. Then the members and attendees get the outputs by mail.

## Governance meeting

"Start a governance meeting" on the circle's page, every member may do
that. Steps: check-in, agenda, closing. The holders of a role start one
on the role's page to break it down into roles: adopting the first role
makes it a circle, with them as its Circle Leads. The tool asks first,
cancelling is the default: changing the role itself is proposed in its
circle.

- **Agenda**: a tension in one or two words, or a proposal (waiting,
  published async, your drafts). "Remove" puts a proposal back: a draft
  stays yours, an async proposal keeps its deadline.
- **Process**: the facilitator chooses an agenda item, the secretary
  clicks it ("Process") and drags the items by their handles to reorder
  them; the steps of the
  integrative decision-making process guide, but don't enforce anything.
- **Proposal**: only the secretary captures it from a tension and changes
  it. "Discard" withdraws it (e.g. it isn't valid, or its proposer is
  gone); its proposer may "Draft again" on the proposal's page. "Done"
  without adopting or discarding keeps it waiting for the next meeting.
- **Objections** are recorded with the person and the role, the
  facilitator tests them, the secretary records it. "Adopt" only works once every objection is
  tested and the valid ones are integrated. The change applies right
  away, the mail comes at the end of the meeting.
- **The same role several times**: if a proposal adopted earlier changed
  a role or policy the current one changes too, the meeting shows the
  conflict before adopting. The secretary opens the role in the proposal
  ("Edit") and merges both versions: text fields contain both between
  markers (`<<<<<<<`, `=======`, `>>>>>>>`), which have to go before
  saving; below the name and the title is the current value, a checkbox
  confirms the merge. If the proposal changes an accountability or domain
  removed in the meantime, its text is among the new ones between
  markers: keep it or delete it. The proposal then starts from the
  current state.
- **Elections**: the facilitator runs the election, the secretary's
  "Record the election" saves the person and the end of the term (Facilitator,
  Secretary, Circle Rep and roles elected by policy). Due elections are
  shown on the circle's page, facilitators get a reminder on Mondays.

## Tactical meeting

- **Regular**: "Start a tactical meeting" on the circle's page, every
  member may do that; all of the circle's roles are invited.
- **Ad hoc**: "Call a tactical meeting" on a role's page, with any roles,
  also from other circles.

Steps: check-in, checklists, metrics, updates, agenda, closing. The
updates show the links of the circle and the invited roles (e.g. to the
task management tool) and the outcomes of the last three tactical
meetings, to check on them ("Show earlier meetings" for more). For each
agenda item the secretary may record an "Outcome" and a "Link", which
save themselves while typing (the others see them), "Save and done" or
just "Done". The results of the completed items stack up on the right
(scroll up for earlier ones), the agenda strikes them through; clicking
one there shows its result, "Reopen" goes back to it to correct it:

- **Nothing** is fine: many items end with information or a short
  discussion.
- **Next actions and projects** stay in the task management tool, the
  tool doesn't track them. Several of them, e.g. one for everybody
  holding a role, are one link to the to-do list containing them.
- **A governance tension** goes into a governance meeting: its owner
  writes it down in a tension ("New proposal" on the circle's page, see [proposals](proposals.md))
  or brings it to the next governance meeting's agenda. The tool can't
  add tensions for somebody else; a to-do reminds them if needed.

The outputs mail at the end lists the items with an outcome or a link.
Tactical meetings imported from GlassFrog have their agenda items only,
GlassFrog's actions and projects aren't imported.
