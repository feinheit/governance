# Meetings

You plan meetings in the calendar. In the tool a meeting begins when
somebody starts it. Everybody follows on a shared screen and adds agenda
items from their own device; the page updates by itself.

- **Who leads**: the facilitator and the secretary. If they're missing,
  others stand in (constitution 5.3.6); both can also be changed during
  the meeting.
- **Attendees** are the circle's members at the start; the facilitator
  and the secretary invite others or remove them.
- **One meeting per circle**: starting a second one leads to the running
  one.
- **End**: "End the meeting", or by itself three hours after the last
  change. Then the members and attendees get the outputs by mail.

## Governance meeting

"Start a governance meeting" on the circle's page, every member may do
that. Steps: check-in, agenda, closing.

- **Agenda**: a tension in one or two words, or a proposal (waiting,
  published async, your drafts).
- **Process**: the facilitator chooses an agenda item; the steps of the
  integrative decision-making process guide, but don't enforce anything.
- **Proposal**: only the secretary captures it from a tension and changes
  it.
- **Objections** are recorded with the person and the role, the
  facilitator tests them. "Adopt" only works once every objection is
  tested and the valid ones are integrated. The change applies right
  away, the mail comes at the end of the meeting.
- **The same role several times**: if a proposal adopted earlier changed
  a role or policy the current one changes too, the meeting shows the
  conflict before adopting. The secretary opens the role in the proposal
  ("Edit") and merges both versions: text fields contain both between
  markers (`<<<<<<<`, `=======`, `>>>>>>>`), which have to go before
  saving; below the name and the title is the current value, a checkbox
  confirms the merge. If the proposal changes an accountability or domain
  removed in the meantime, its text is among the new ones between
  markers: keep it or delete it. The proposal then starts from the
  current state.
- **Elections**: the facilitator runs the election, "Record the
  election" saves the person and the end of the term (Facilitator,
  Secretary, Circle Rep and roles elected by policy). Due elections are
  shown on the circle's page, secretaries get a reminder on Mondays.

## Tactical meeting

- **Regular**: the facilitator or the secretary starts it on the circle's
  page, all of the circle's roles are invited.
- **Ad hoc**: "Call a tactical meeting" on a role's page, with any roles,
  also from other circles.

Steps: check-in, checklists, metrics, updates, agenda, closing. For each
agenda item the secretary records the outcome, a next action or a
project, and the link to the task created for it in the task management
tool.
